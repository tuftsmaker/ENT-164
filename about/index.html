Organizations Thomas has worked with and for

Thomas is a technology executive and educator with more than 20 years of experience leading digital transformations. At Accenture he built digital engineering teams for retailers like Best Buy; at Amazon he led global AI-driven operations and co-founded Amazon Fresh stores and Amazon Locker. Today he is the founder of TeachPilot and a lecturer in Entrepreneurship & AI Innovation at Tufts University, focused on democratizing engineering education through AI-powered, project-based learning.

He brings the same build-test-learn loop he used to ship billion-dollar products into the classroom — teaching students to design, fabricate, wire, program, and iterate until their ideas work in the real world.

"As a pilot I learned that simulators don't replace flight time — they make every hour in the cockpit count." Thomas Van de Velde, on the idea behind TeachPilot
