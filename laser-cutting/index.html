What's inside

From file to finished cut

Thirteen steps, with a screenshot of every dialog you will meet.

01

Install and import

Get Inkscape, open your DXF, and choose the scaling that keeps your part the size you designed.

02

Set the colours

Hairline strokes, no fill, and the exact RGB values the laser reads: red cuts, blue scores, black engraves.

03

Set up UCP

Pick your material and thickness, and set power and speed from the whiteboard rather than the database.

04

Position and cut

Line the job up with your material using Focus View, focus the laser, check the corners, then press play.

Never leave the laser unattended. Sometimes the laser sets your material on fire. There is a fire blanket on the side of each laser — it works well, but only if you are standing there to use it.
Based on the official Nolop guide. This walkthrough follows the Nolop Makerspace laser cutting guide, adapted for ENT-164 with Inkscape replacing Adobe Illustrator. The UCP screenshots come from that guide, used under CC BY-SA.
