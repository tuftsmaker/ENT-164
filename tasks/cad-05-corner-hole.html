Task 5 · Laser-Ready File

Adding a Circle at a Corner

A 100 × 60 mm rectangle with one 10 mm diameter hole whose centre is 20 mm from each of the two edges meeting the top-right corner.

72 video One Onshape sketch Signed off by a TA
Watch first. Adding a Circle at a Corner (72).

What to do

Anchor a hole to a corner instead of the middle, using a construction line and dimensions from the two edges that meet there.

Do first: Placing a Circle in the Center of a Rectangle.

What to hand in

FileWhat it is
part.dxfrequired
manifest.mdrequired

Put both files in one folder, run the checker, and upload the zip in Canvas. Ask opencode: check my cad-01 submission.

How it is checked

Every criterion below is checked on your file. The ones marked for a person are judged by a TA — they are never passed or failed by the checker.

Checked automatically

Every line end meets another line end. A corner that only looks joined would cut open.
