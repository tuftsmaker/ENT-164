Qualification

Laser-Ready File

Six tasks that take you from a blank Onshape document to a DXF you can hand to the laser cutter at Nolop. Each task is a short video, one file, and a signoff.

6 tasks Six DXFs One supervised cut

The tasks

Every task file must pass its checks, and a TA signs each one. Then a TA or instructor signs the qualification as a whole, after you cut one of these files in class under supervision.

Then: Supervised cut

Bring one of your DXF files to Nolop and cut it with a TA or staff member present. This is the only part of the qualification that cannot be checked from a file.

This qualification says your file is ready for the laser. It does not authorize you to run the laser — that is Nolop's own training and checkout, and it is separate on purpose.
