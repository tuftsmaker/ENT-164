8 tasks, each building on the last. Follow the arrows — a task unlocks once the ones pointing at it are signed off.

Every task is one video and one file. The last step is not a file at all: you cut one of your own parts at Nolop, with a TA watching. That is what turns 8 signed tasks into the qualification.
