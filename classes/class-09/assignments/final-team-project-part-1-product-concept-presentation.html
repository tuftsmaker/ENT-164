Concept Proposal

At this stage, you are not required to build the final prototype. Instead, your presentation should highlight how the following requirements will be addressed in the final product:

  • Solves a unique problem
  • Applies core maker techniques:
    • Laser cutting
    • 3D printing
    • Microcontroller with sensors/actuators
    • Smart API Integration (e.g. AI or other external integration)
  • Size constraint: 40cm x 25cm x 25cm (fit in the box)

Product Concept Presentation

  • Problem & Opportunity – Clear articulation of the problem and its importance.
  • Proposed Product Concept – A walkthrough of the concept, including how you will meet the final product requirements.
    • Sketches
    • Foam core or other mockup
  • Next Steps – What you will build and test in the second half of the semester.
