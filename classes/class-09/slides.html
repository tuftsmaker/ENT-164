Final project requirements

What your build must include

Your concept proposal has to show how you'll hit every one of these.

01

Laser cutting

At least one part designed as a 2D cut — and the proposal says where it fits in the build.

02

3D printing

Printed parts where they make sense: mounts, brackets, and custom shapes.

03

Microcontroller + I/O

A microcontroller with sensors and actuators, so the product senses the world and responds.

04

Smart API integration

AI or another external service wired into the device — a core maker skill.

The brief

Solves a unique problem — and fits in the project box: 40 × 25 × 25 cm. Your proposal should clearly indicate which parts use each method and how they contribute to the design.

** Cannot be an automatic plant-watering system :)

How we'll decide

Note-and-Vote: silent ideas, fast decision

01 · NOTE

Ideas first, alone

Everyone writes their own ideas quietly, one per note, so the loudest voice doesn't set the agenda.

02 · VOTE

Vote with dots

Post the notes, then vote in silence with round stickers. The heatmap shows where the team's energy really is.

Voice for everyone, fast decision — then the team discusses the top ideas together.

ENT-164 · Intro to MakingClass 9 · Final Project Kickoff08

In class today

From sticky notes to a team concept

Step 1

Individually · 10 min · in silence

Write ideas

  • Write 3 unique ideas
  • One idea per sticky note
  • Each idea must meet the criteria

Step 2

As a team · 5 min · in silence

Post & vote

  • Post every idea on the team wall
  • Vote for your top 3 with round stickers — you can vote for your own

Step 3

As a team · 20 min

Discuss & decide

  • Count the votes
  • Discuss the top 3 ideas
  • Decide what you'll pursue
Then

Go directly to Nolop and start the concept presentation — sketch, mockup, and slides.
