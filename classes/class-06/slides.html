Two ways to connect · 1 of 2

Direct — the board’s own hotspot

The board becomes the network: your phone joins it, and nothing else is needed.

01

The board is the network

The ESP32 creates its own WiFi hotspot. Your phone joins it like any network — no router involved.

02

Join it, open the page

The board serves the page at its own address — like 192.168.4.1. Type it in and the controls are there.

03

Simple, but offline

While your phone is on the board’s network it is off the internet — normal, and fine for class.

ENT-164 · Intro to MakingClass 6 · Going Wireless05

Two ways to connect · 2 of 2

Shared — through the internet

The board joins a network you already have, and you reach it over that network.

01

One network, two devices

The board joins a WiFi — home, or a shared one — and gets an address on it, like any computer.

02

Reach it from anywhere on the network

Any device on that network can open the board’s page — and your phone keeps its internet while you control it.

03

It also gets the internet

On a shared network the board can reach online services — and it obeys the network’s rules, like its password.

On your phone

Every move is a request

The page has two controls: the stick drives, the button works the servo.

What each control does

Drag the stick → the motors follow your thumb
Let go → everything stops
Press the button → the servo opens or closes

Every drag and press is a request the board answers itself. The whole app is one page — nothing to install.

ENT-164 · Intro to MakingClass 6 · Going Wireless14

Under the hood

What happened?

Every tap on your phone becomes a request the ESP32 answers.

1

A web server starts

Your ESP32 runs a web server that renders a web page. No separate computer needed.

2

The page gets an address

The board serves the page from the network it creates — the address the code prints, like http://192.168.4.1.

3

Your phone renders it

Your phone's browser renders the HTML that the ESP32's web server generates.

4

Requests round-trip

Interacting with the page sends a request to the ESP32 (its address is http://192.168.4.1). It reads the request, sets the pins, and answers with the next page state.
