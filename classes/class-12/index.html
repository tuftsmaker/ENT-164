Plan the session

Workshop time goes fast. Come in with a list and leave with a working prototype.

01

Finish the build

The physical thing: mount what is loose, fix what is broken, and clean up the wiring.

02

Make it reliable

Run it more than once. If it only works on the first try, that is the job for today.

03

Rehearse the demo

Decide who presents, what you'll show, and the order you'll show it in.

Use the whole session. This is the last class before Demo Day — the next time your project runs, it runs in front of an audience.

Ready for Demo Day

A useful checklist for the last hour of the session.

The build

It works, twice

  • Powers on and runs from a clean start
  • No loose wires or unsecured parts
  • You know what to do if it misbehaves

The story

Someone can explain it

  • The problem you chose, in one sentence
  • What you built, and what it does
  • What you'd do next with more time

The setup

It travels

  • Everything fits in one box or bag
  • Bring the charger or batteries
  • Have the presentation ready to open

Getting help

Instructors and TAs are on the floor for the whole session.

Stuck? Say what you expected, what happened, and what you already tried. That is usually enough to solve it in a couple of minutes.
