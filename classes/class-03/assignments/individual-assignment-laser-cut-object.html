Objective:

Design and create an interesting laser cut object. This is your chance to be creative and explore what’s possible with the laser cutter.

Instructions:

  1. Choose or Design Your Project

    • You may search for an existing laser cut project template online (e.g., free vector files).

    • Or you may design your own original project using Onshape (or another vector design tool).

  2. Prepare Your Files

    • Ensure your design is in a vector format suitable for the laser cutter (e.g., .SVG, .DXF, or .AI).

    • Organize and save all files you used in the design process.

  3. Fabricate and Assemble

    • Use the laser cutter to cut your design.

    • Assemble your project into its final form (gluing, slotting, or fastening as needed).

  4. Submit Your Work

    • Upload your vector design files (the cutting files you used).

    • Upload photos of your finished project (both in-progress and completed).

  5. Bring to Class

    • Bring your assembled laser cut object to class for presentation and discussion.
