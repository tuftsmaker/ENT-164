Goal

Build your own web site that integrates with your ESP32 and an external API. It cannot be a weather site, including the one we built in class. Pick something you would actually use or show someone.

What it must include

  1. A device that is part of the product. Your ESP32 sends something real to the site, acts on something the site sends, or both. A sensor, an LED, a motor, a display.
  2. A deployed web site. A URL anyone can open, showing something that is true about your device.
  3. An external API. Something your site calls that you could not compute yourself: weather, transit, a model, a public dataset.

How to build it

Use opencode, and work in stages. Get the device reading or acting on its own first, then the upload, then the page, then the API. Push to main as you go: Vercel rebuilds the site on every push, so the live URL always matches your repository.

Keep the shape from class: the board posts to your own API, the database keeps the readings, the page shows them, and one API call adds something new.

What to submit

Site URL: the deployed address, working, with your device's data on it.

Demo: a short video, under 2 minutes, with the device and the page in the same shot so the connection is visible.

Conversation: a share link to your opencode session, showing the prompts, what broke, and how you described the problem back.

Reflection:

  • What surprised you? Describe a moment where the result did not match your expectation and how that changed your approach.
  • When did you get stuck, and what did you try before it clicked?
  • What would you do differently if you started over tomorrow?

Grading (10 pts)
Working site with the device's data 4
External API used for something real 2
Demo shows the device and the page together 2
Conversation and reflection 2

Tips

  • Be specific with the agent: name the board, the pin, the endpoint, and paste the exact error.
  • If a key is involved, keep it in Vercel's environment variables, never in the repository.
  • Build the smallest version that works end to end, then add to it.
