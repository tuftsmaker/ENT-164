Objective:

Design and fabricate an interesting object using 3D printing. This assignment is about learning the digital-to-physical workflow and experimenting with form, function, and creativity.

Instructions:

  1. Choose or Design Your Project

    • You may download an existing 3D model from free repositories (e.g., Thingiverse, Printables).

    • You may create your own original design using Onshape or another CAD program.

    • Or, you may sculpt a model with clay and scan it into a 3D file using the Polycam app (or another 3D scanning tool).

  2. Prepare Your Files

    • Save your design as a 3D-printable file (e.g., .STL or .OBJ).

    • Slice your file into a format suitable for the 3D printer (e.g., .gcode).

  3. Fabricate Your Object

    • Print your object on the 3D printer.

    • If your design requires multiple pieces, print and assemble them into the final form.

  4. Submit Your Work

    • Upload your 3D design files (CAD, STL, or scan files).

    • Upload photos of your 3D printed object (both in-progress and final).

  5. Bring to Class

    • Bring your finished 3D printed object to class for presentation and discussion.
