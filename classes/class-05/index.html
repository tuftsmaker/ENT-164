Your kit & board

Freenove FNK0046 Super Starter Kit for ESP32 WROVER — everything you need for the electronics project.

The brain

ESP32-WROVER board, GPIO extension board, and a solderless breadboard.

The basics

Jumper wires, LEDs, resistors (220Ω, 1K, 10K), push buttons, USB cable.

The fun stuff

LCD and 7-segment displays, buzzers, joystick, potentiometers, servo, TT motor + wheel, motor driver, ultrasonic sensor, camera.

No paper tutorial. The download link for the tutorial and code is on the box.

Wiring basics

No soldering — push parts and wires into the holes and they connect. Learn the breadboard rules before you plug anything in.

Rows

Each row of five holes is connected horizontally, and the center channel separates the two sides.

Power rails

The vertical rails along the edges carry 5V, 3.3V, and GND to every row.

Pin rows

Your ESP32 plugs into the GPIO extension board: any of the horizontal holes in a pin's row connects to that pin — for example, pin 34.

Before you wire: remove the pre-installed demo parts from the breadboard, seat the ESP32 and extension board across the center channel, and read the pin labels — 5V, 3V3, GND, and the numbered GPIO pins.

MicroPython & AI

Your ESP32 runs MicroPython — a tiny version of Python. Install it once, then let AI help you write the code.

One-time setup: follow the instructions at go.tufts.edu/esp32 to put the snake on the plane — flash MicroPython onto your ESP32, then connect with Thonny.

Your first program — blink.py

from machine import Pin
import time

led = Pin(2, Pin.OUT)

while True:
    led.on()        # Turn LED on
    time.sleep(1)   # Wait 1 second
    led.off()       # Turn LED off
    time.sleep(1)   # Wait 1 second

Challenge: can you make it blink faster or slower?
