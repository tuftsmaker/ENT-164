Purpose. Reflection is how making turns into learning: you stop, look at what you actually did with your hands, and find out what it taught you.

This is a short written reflection on today's physical making — the electronics build. Answer these three prompts, a few sentences each:

  • Catch the surprise. Find the moment the build didn't go the way you pictured it. What did you notice, and what did you do next?
  • Think on your feet. Point to a decision you made mid-build without stopping to plan it. What were you reacting to?
  • Look back. Building it again tomorrow: what would you keep exactly as it is, and what would you change — and why?

The prompts follow Donald Schön's reflective practice: reflection in action, and reflection on action.

Where to submit: go.tufts.edu/reflect, by the end of the day it is due. Reflections are part of your participation grade.
