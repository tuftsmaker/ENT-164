Purpose. Reflection is how making turns into learning: you stop, look at what you actually did with your hands, and find out what it taught you.

This is a short written reflection on the physical making so far — foam core mockups, laser cutting, and 3D printing. Answer these three prompts, a few sentences each:

  • Catch the surprise. Find a moment across the three builds when the material didn't do what you pictured. What did you notice, and what did you do next?
  • Think on your feet. Point to a decision you made mid-build without stopping to plan it. What were you reacting to?
  • Look back. Across the three projects: what would you keep exactly as it is, and what would you change — and why?

How to write it. Ground every answer in a specific moment from your builds. A few honest paragraphs beat a polished essay.

Where to submit: Upload your reflection as a one-page document to this assignment on Canvas, by the end of the day it is due. Reflections are part of your participation grade.
