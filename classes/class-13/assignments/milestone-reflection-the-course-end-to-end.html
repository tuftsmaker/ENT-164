Purpose. Reflection is the engine of learning in project-based work: a structured pause to examine what you did, what it taught you, and what you will do next.

This is the milestone reflection on the whole course, end to end. Answer these, a few sentences each:

  • What worked well, and why?
  • What would you do differently?
  • What did you learn about yourself?

Where to submit: go.tufts.edu/reflect, by the end of the day it is due. Reflections are part of your participation grade.
