This final peer review provides an opportunity to reflect on your team's collaboration for the final project and give constructive feedback that will help everyone grow as future teammates and professionals.

  • Recognize your teammates' contributions
  • Provide constructive feedback for growth
  • Reflect on what made your team effective (or what could have improved)

Instructions:

  • Complete one peer review form for each of your teammates (do not review yourself).
  • Select a teammate's name from the dropdown menu before beginning each review.

Example: If your team has 5 members total, you will submit 4 peer review forms (one for each teammate).

Grading (5 points total):

  • 2 points: Completion of all required peer reviews
  • 3 points: Average of the ratings you receive from your teammates
