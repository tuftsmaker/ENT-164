On demo day, the final product demonstration is the centerpiece of your presentation. You'll showcase your work in front of class peers and guests. To set the context for your product demo and explain how it works internally, you are invited to present a short presentation following this suggested outline:

Problem Statement

  • Define the customer problem or market pain
  • Explain why current solutions are inadequate

Solution

  • Describe how your product solves the problem
  • Highlight key features and benefits

Product Evolution

  • Explain how the product works and how it evolved from its initial concept. Refer back to initial sketches and photos of earlier iterations to showcase what you've learned along the way.

Technical Design Drawings

  • Illustrate the technical design with diagrams showing the various components and connections.

Final Product Expectations:

Your final product must incorporate and demonstrate the following maker techniques:

  • Laser cutting
  • 3D printing
  • Microcontroller with sensors/actuators
  • Smart API Integration (e.g. AI or other external integration)

    Size constraint: 40cm x 25cm x 25cm (fit in the box)

Ensure fit and finish:

  • The final product should be well-assembled and tested.
  • Wires should be neatly organized and tucked away.
  • Microcontrollers and other technical components should be properly housed within the product, minimizing visual distractions for the end-user.

Grading Criteria:

Your final project will be graded as follows:

  • Showcasing Key Maker Techniques: 60%
  • Fit and Finish: 20%
  • Effectiveness at Addressing the Problem Domain: 20%

Submission Requirements:

  • Provide a link to your final presentation.
  • Upload your final product design files (e.g., CAD files, SVG files, and MicroPython source code).
Bring your product in its project container the day of the demo. We will collect the project boxes at the end of demo day.
