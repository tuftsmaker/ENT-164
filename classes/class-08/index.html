Race day

Class 8 is the finish line for the first module: a working robot, a full scoreboard, and the awards.

01

Bring the robot

Fully assembled, wired, and charged — with your dispenser if your team built one.

02

Race it

Each team runs its robot in the challenge arena and moves as many balls as possible between the two lines.

03

Close it out

Scores are tallied on the board, and the awards go to the teams behind this semester's robots.

This is a team day. Everyone on the team should be able to explain what the robot does, how it is built, and what you would change. That is what the reflection asks for.

How it's scored

The challenge is deliberately simple to describe and hard to execute — which is the point.

The task

Move the balls

  • Two lines on the floor, one pile of balls
  • Robot, dispenser, and drive team only

The score

Balls between the lines

  • Counted at the end of the run
  • Highest score wins

The real test

It has to work twice

  • Reliability beats a lucky run
  • Serviceable wiring survives the day
Rules change every year. The exact arena, ball count, and scoring for this term are in the assignment on Canvas.
