What to Do

Complete one peer review for each teammate (don't review yourself—you'll complete a separate self-evaluation form)

Example: 5-person team = 4 peer reviews

Link: https://forms.gle/fvmwhrUmvWUn9DeC8

Instructions

1. Click the link and select a teammate's name
2. Rate their contributions and provide specific feedback
3. Submit the form
4. Repeat for each remaining teammate

Tips for Good Feedback

  • Be specific: "Led our design brainstorm and created the prototype" not "good worker"
  • Be constructive: Suggest what would help, not just what's wrong
  • Be honest: Your feedback helps teammates grow

Grading

2 points – Complete all required peer reviews by the deadline

Note: You'll also receive 3 points based on ratings from your teammates (total = 5 points)

Confidentiality

Your feedback will be summarized and anonymized before being shared with your teammates. Each person will receive a combined summary of all peer reviews, with no individual responses attributed to specific reviewers. This ensures honest feedback while protecting your privacy.
