The session What to bring Next week

A full working session

There is no new tool this week. The class is the build: assemble, test, fix, and repeat.

01

Assemble

Mount the motors, the boards, the battery, and the tracks. A part that can shift under load is not mounted yet.

02

Test

Drive it, load it, and run it until something fails. A robot that works on the bench has not been tested yet.

03

Fix and re-test

Change one thing at a time, then run it again. Write down what you changed, so the next fault is easier to find.

Split the work. One person drives, one watches the wiring and the joints, one keeps the notes. Swap roles halfway through so everyone knows the whole robot.

What to bring

Come with everything you have made so far. Class time is for assembly and testing.

  • The robot so far: frame, tracks, and any parts already mounted
  • Your 3D-printed parts and laser-cut panels
  • The electronics kit: board, motor driver, motors, and battery
  • Your sketches, measurements, and any notes from the build
  • Any part you are unsure about, so the team can check it together
Charge the battery before class. Testing time disappears fast when the robot stops halfway through a run. Bring the charger too.
A part that does not fit is normal at this stage. Bring it anyway. Sometimes it needs a small change, and sometimes it becomes the reason the design gets better.
