Why this setup

Built for making things with AI

OpenCode is the app, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is the brain, and OpenRouter is the connection. Here is why the class uses them.

01

Built for this work

Designed for reasoning and agentic coding with reliable tool use, so it can genuinely help with your class projects.

02

Open weights

The model itself is openly licensed and inspectable — which fits a making class better than a closed black box.

03

Big context, tiny cost

It can read entire files — up to 1M tokens — for a fraction of a cent per question, so the shared class budget lasts the term.

04

One connection

One OpenRouter account and one key. Explore other models later without starting over.

Step by step

The whole setup, at a glance

Each step has screenshots and plain-English instructions in the full guide.

  1. 1

    Download the OpenCode app

    Go to opencode.ai/download and click Download for macOS.

  2. 2

    Install the app

    Drag OpenCode onto your Applications folder, then open it like any Mac app.

  3. 3

    Join your class on OpenRouter

    Sign up at openrouter.ai with your Tufts email and request to join the class workspace.

  4. 4

    Create your own API key

    Name it OpenCode, copy it once, and keep it private — it starts with sk-or-.

  5. 5

    Connect OpenRouter in the app

    Open the model panel, choose OpenRouter, paste your key, and click Continue.

  6. 6

    Choose DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

    Search v4.1 flash, switch it on in Manage models if needed, and select it.

  7. 7

    Ask your first question

    Type in plain English and press Enter. That's it — you're making with AI.

  8. ✓

    Read the full guide

    Open the illustrated setup guide → for screenshots, tips, and troubleshooting.
