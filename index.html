Before you start

What you'll need

Almost nothing. If you can install an app, you can follow any of these guides.

Checklist

  • An Apple laptop running macOS 13 or newer, or a PC running Windows 10 or 11
  • An internet connection
  • Your Tufts email — some class services are tied to the tufts.edu domain
  • Nothing to pay — the class budget covers AI usage for your coursework
Use your Tufts email. Class workspaces and budgets are tied to the tufts.edu domain. Signing up with a different email means you won't be offered them.
Keep the budget healthy. The class budget is shared, so keep questions focused on class work and avoid long, off-topic chats.
Stuck on something? Every guide ends with a troubleshooting table — and your instructor is happy to help.
